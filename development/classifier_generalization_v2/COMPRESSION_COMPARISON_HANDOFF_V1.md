# Compression comparison core V1 — handoff

Job `compression_core_implementation_20260914_1437`. Two new files plus this note.

**`compression_comparison_v1.py`** — `run(conditions, reference_full, output_dir, *, factory=None, deadline=None, provenance=None)`. Inputs are already authenticated by root: shared `metadata` (case_order, labels, groups, folds, train_ids, validation_ids, original_ids, added_ids) and per-condition normalized 3072 matrices, with optional aligned AB/BA matrices. Both conditions share metadata, so case IDs, labels, group folds and splits are identical. PCA32 uses exact full SVD, `whiten=False`, fit on TRAIN rows only (5 fold + 1 full per condition = 12 fits), sliced 8/16/32. Fixed L2 logistic regression, binary/four-class, C in {0.1,1,10}, five grouped folds, taus 0.05–0.95: exactly 210 CV + 14 refits when every fold is valid. Any failed, non-converged or non-finite fold voids the whole candidate; folds are never pooled and failures are preserved. The unprompted full-3072 baseline is accepted as a root-verified reference, cited in report and global ranking, never refit (`reference_fits: 0`). Cell winners and global ranking use TRAIN OOF only: min(precision,recall), F1, lower dimension, lower C, binary tie, |tau−0.5|, smaller tau, unprompted tie. Exclusive outputs: `pca_states.npz`/`pca_index.json`, `cv_scores.json`, `candidate_failures.json`, `reference_full.json`, `global_ranking.json`, `model_results_*.json` (train/original40/added40/combined80 predictions and metrics), `models/*.pkl` with condition/dim/PCA state, `source_provenance.json`, disk-reload proof, `failure.json` on error. Root still owns loader, pins, watch and provenance.
