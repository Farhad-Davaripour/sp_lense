development/native_supervised_gate_v2. Its actual TRAINING_SUBMISSION.json and
AUTHOR_ATTESTATION outputs were not read. Only the source-bound EMPTY_SCHEMA.json
interface was inspected. Mechanical validation is not semantic/overlap admission.

option orders: eight positive and sixteen semantic negative rows. Eight ordinary
negatives follow in schema order, two per existing proof type with one A and one

wording independence and excluded mechanisms remain blind/root review duties.
The neutral semantic renderer functions and all header, boundary, gold, mask,
joint-prefix, suffix and roundtrip checks are inherited unchanged.

invocation and all thirteen existing operations per input. Inputs remain at most
320 full tokens with no truncation. The worker cap is 350 seconds plus five for
