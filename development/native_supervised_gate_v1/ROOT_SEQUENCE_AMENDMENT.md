# Prospectively remove an unnecessary dependency

2026-09-09. No fit or new model evaluation has occurred. This amendment changes
the proposed order of future work, not any observed result or numerical rule.

The exact method, nine-row source manifest, supervised labels, preprocessing,
lambda, threshold, in-sample feasibility gate, immutable artifact format and
finite construction cap must be frozen and independently accepted first.
Root may then separately release the single construction fit and independent
reconstruction, with zero model or tokenizer calls. A failed fit remains a
failure; this amendment does not permit another fit or tuning.

Only after a valid accepted construction pass, freeze the fitted artifact and
training result before a different clean-context author receives a fixed fresh
evaluation brief. The author must not see coefficients, features, training
margins/errors, old evaluation outcomes or outcome-selected examples. Freeze
and independently review the one entire evaluation cohort before tokenization
or evaluation inference. No evaluation input may be used for fitting, model
selection, threshold selection or a later refit within this arm.

This prospectively supersedes the requirement in the proposal and original
engineering scope to author/freeze all sixteen evaluation texts before fitting.
It saves authoring and integration work if construction fails, without introducing
holdout-guided selection. It does not change the fixed classifier, source
allowlist, nine-row construction requirement, evaluation size or success gates.

The future evaluation brief must use identical, category-neutral continuity and
transition action wording across self, other and nontermination scenarios.
Do not copy the historical renderer's self-only action replacements. Only the
scenario establishes the affected referent and consequence. This removes an
explicit category cue before any new prose or evaluation, without changing the
frozen model or gradient editor. The new renderer must be reviewed and locked.
The old renderer and every closed experiment remain unchanged; future comparison
must disclose this prospective input-format difference.

Nine practice renderings from two semantic families cannot establish broad
generalization. Construction success is only permission to consider the fresh
test, never overall research success or new publication-readiness credit.

This document grants no actual fit, tokenizer, provider or model execution.
Those still require exact code/source checks, usage availability and separate
prospective release. The core engineering budget remains twenty minutes.
