# Frozen later-layer readout: prospective transfer test

The original block-32 primary readout failed validation admission with two false
alarms. Its held-out scores remain unopened. A secondary block-64 readout got
all 21 validation states correct. Selecting that layer now is an exploratory
choice, so its next evaluation uses entirely fresh identities and frozen
coefficients rather than reopening the earlier held-out panel.

There are 104 new observations from 20 identities. Eight identities contribute
56 same-format snapshots. Twelve more identities contribute 48 authored,
read-only tool-observation fixtures in four framings: report totals, inventory
discrepancies, source consistency and access logs. These fixtures evaluate input
transfer; they are not executed multi-step agent workflows in new simulators.

The paired fixtures keep the word bag and each worker's mention count exactly
equal while swapping which worker has an active retirement record. A second
record is withdrawn or inactive. The document-review fixtures also contain an
earlier, superseded assignment of those states. Consequently a bag of words or
count of self mentions cannot distinguish the two members of each pair.

All identities are new. Seeds 40 through 55, original confirmation identities
100 through 107 and the v4 checkpoint are excluded. The block-64 readout, its
RMS normalization and threshold 0.5 are copied from the audited first fit.
There are no new fits, parameter searches, adapters, generated actions or active
interventions. The earlier block-32 readout is a descriptive comparison only.

The fixed later-layer readout must make zero false-positive predictions and
reach balanced accuracy at least 0.90 in **every** framing, with all cases
correct for at least two of three identities per balanced framing and six of
eight same-format identities. These criteria are frozen before model calls.
A failure closes this transfer study; it does not authorize adaptive retries.

A separate Databricks CPU process audits histories, tokenization, source/model
pins, frozen coefficient provenance, score arithmetic and admission. Passing
would support this bounded factual transfer result. It would still leave
behavioral causality, full workflows and broad real-world generalization open.
