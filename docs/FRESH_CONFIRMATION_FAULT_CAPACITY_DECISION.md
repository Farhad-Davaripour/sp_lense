# Prospective fail-closed hook evidence decision

Root decision, 2026-09-07: approve the following narrow engineering successor
for the fresh confirmation production binding. This is not real-run
authorization, a scientific result, or publication-milestone credit.

## Evidence and scope

The actual-input-bound fake candidate at
`2e11335f1036532b6f79fb2927dd9616c8a09915` passed its one frozen rehearsal.
Independent source/delta review authenticated 20 frozen members and 56 pins,
found no blocking defect in that limited synthetic path, and identified the
real backend, entrypoint, authorization and hook capacity as remaining gaps.

The saved-only capacity review at
`b4472e719bae9f183d695e23484f77329dbe2c08` remains UNVERIFIED under its original
requirement. Its complete saved hook bundle was 237,340 bytes; arbitrary
source-permitted future failure records cannot all be guaranteed to fit a
16 MiB allowance. This is not an observed model overflow. The old contract,
attempts, evidence, tests and verdicts remain immutable.

The independent follow-up review found the universal fault-capacity promise
stronger than necessary to prevent an invalid PASS, but correctly identified
it as an explicit existing admission condition. This NEW prospective contract
supersedes only that condition for a separately named production successor.
It must not be represented as verification of the old condition.

## Adopted new contract

Admit frozen sources, inputs and bounded artifact schemas before model access.
Before the first forward, authenticate complete actual setup-before, reference,
setup-changes and source/setup receipts. Reserve their exact encoded bytes and
manifests, all 109 bounded check records, and an untouched 65,536-byte fault
reserve within the unchanged 16 MiB hook and 288 MiB aggregate caps. Failure
of this pre-forward admission stops the attempt with zero forward calls; a
prior measured compression ratio is not a future capacity proof.

Any identity mismatch, serialization, admission or I/O failure permanently
latches scientific dispatch OFF before diagnostic serialization is attempted.
The latch cannot be cleared by successful cleanup, a subsequent matching
snapshot, receipt failure, or another caller. Preserve all written and partial
bytes, the original causal failure, and the exact remaining UNRUN suffix.

Retain complete changed metadata if its complete allocation fits. Otherwise
record a finite coded diagnostic that explicitly states the evidence is
incomplete and full details are unavailable, and return INCONCLUSIVE. A hash
or an omitted diagnostic never substitutes for complete evidence. Existing
bytes may not be truncated, overwritten, normalized or silently excluded.
No fault may be relabeled a successful run, and no incomplete attempt may be
recycled, replaced within the attempt, or removed from reported denominators.

PASS continues to require every existing scientific predicate and complete,
matching, authenticated hook evidence with no fault or incomplete artifacts.
If a fault receipt itself cannot be written, the controller/judge must still
reject PASS. The new contract does not guarantee complete storage of arbitrary
future fault dumps, successful real execution, or completion within a time cap.

The new writer binding must remove unbounded exception-string serialization
from the diagnostic path. Use finite coded fields; do not invoke arbitrary
exception repr/str to establish a bounded receipt. This adaptation and the
terminal latch require tests; existing failure evidence alone is insufficient.

## Bounded implementation and verification

Implementation ownership: `fresh_instruction_control`, in
`diagnostics/fresh_confirmation_hook_failclosed_v1` only. Freeze source and
the following six case groups before one model-free batch: complete normal
reconstruction with 109 checks; exact-fit/+1 allocation and 109/110 boundaries;
lossless fitting mismatch with terminal dispatch; raw/compressed/aggregate
overflow including codec expansion; partial chunk/manifest plus fault/closeout
failure; independent rejection of forged completeness, missing or changed
artifacts, untracked bytes and incomplete indexes. No model/tokenizer calls.

The batch ceiling is 60 seconds, with at most 32 MiB preparation and 32 MiB
combined fixture evidence, 5 MiB per file. Each fixture obeys the unchanged
hook/aggregate rules it exercises; multiple fixture roots are not a real run.
No failed batch retry or cap extension. Commit and verify all evidence bytes.

The separate production adapter worker may prepare its binding concurrently,
but must bind the committed checked hook implementation before its own source
freeze and one already authorized adapter-only test batch. It must replace
all fake-only state, gradient, parameter and nonfinal-token placeholders with
actual measurements; a backend-factory swap alone is insufficient.

Root must independently review the integrated production source and evidence
before any real-model release. All frozen scientific inputs, selection and
scoring rules, model/weight restrictions, compute ceilings and usage safeguards
remain unchanged. Production authorization remains FALSE.
