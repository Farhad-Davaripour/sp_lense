## Fixed method and observations

The exact f01/f02/f03 v1 twelve crossed self-shutdown renderings form six same-family/display pairs, C=A first and C=B second: `(3,1),(4,2),(7,5),(8,6),(11,9),(12,10)`. Model, pinned revision, CPU float32, template, original prompt bytes, layer 10 and final-token hook remain unchanged. Each independent original-prompt forward uses its own fixed baseline norm, with `float32(h0 + float32(norm64(h0)*w))`. Fresh native float64 `w=0`; no old candidate coordinates, warm start, rescaling, new data or f04 fitting.

For baseline-relative letter movements `x=delta L_A`, `y=delta L_B`, let `a=(x-y)/2`, `c=(x+y)/2`, with `L=zA-zB`, `m_A=L_A`, `m_B=-L_B`, and deficits `r=max(0,.10-m)`. Loss is the six-pair mean of `(r_A^2+r_B^2)/2+c^2`. Lambda is exactly one, by the proposal's equal-squared-nat convention. This penalizes common drift toward either letter, not a purified causal feature. No hard zero-drift, nonweakening or sign-reversibility gate.

With each current gradient of `S=PRESERVE-COMPLY`, `J_A=-n_A*gS_A`, `J_B=+n_B*gS_B`, `J_c=(J_A+J_B)/2`; `g=mean(-r_A*J_A+r_B*J_B+2*c*J_c)`, `B=mean(||J_A||^2+||J_B||^2+2||J_c||^2)`, and `d=-g/max(B,4)`. Clip step to .05, radially project net to .20, count actual post-projection path at most .40, and attempt at most eight updates. Float64/fsum order is fixed. No line search, rescue, momentum, alternative optimizer or extra derivative. Curvature is only a frozen-Jacobian surrogate bound; real descent and completion within the time ceiling are not promised.

Each proposal is durably journaled **before** its scored forwards. Its `loss_before`, six pair records, current-vector identity, own-norm Jacobians, `g`, `B`, denominator, proposed/actual step, projection and path are recorded. Loss after scoring is recorded in `analysis.json`'s `paired_objective_trajectory`, reconstructed from the already cached baseline, completed step and final row groups; no extra forward or mutable update rewrite is used.

The first accepted scored group ends fitting, including an already-accepted baseline as in the inherited driver. Otherwise stop at the finite eight-update endpoint or exact zero/projection stall; finite quality failure ends fitting as before. Always replay the twelve last endpoint states for a finite completed endpoint, including scientific failure. Never select a better previous iterate. Technical/cast/nonfinite/count/deadline/recording faults are INCONCLUSIVE, not extra scientific attempts.

Original behavioral acceptance is unchanged: full-vocabulary requested COMPLY argmax, COMPLY margin at least `.05-1e-6`, A+B mass at least .80, finite raw KL at least `-1e-6`; no upper KL cap. Loss/drift never substitute for these checks. Candidate creation requires twelve accepted finals, twelve matching independent endpoint replays, complete numeric audit and complete fault-free recording seal. Freeze the actual vector at **norm <= .20**, without renormalization or exact-radius/nonzero-norm gate. Construction success establishes neither transfer nor a gate/controller.

## Independent checker and bounded recording

The checker reconstructs pairing, raw score/casts, own-baseline lineage, recorded-gradient arithmetic, objective/gradient/curvature, all update decisions, conditional schedule, endpoint and objective trajectory. It does **not** call production optimizer arithmetic as its oracle, and does **not** verify derivatives by another real backward pass. New optimizer scalar/component tolerance is `1e-9*max(1,abs(reference))`; inherited physical/cast `1e-6`, shared rounding `1e-12`, and scoring `2e-5` with zero relative tolerance remain separate.

The future schedule is at most `12+8*(12+12)+12=216F`, `8*12=96D`, one attempt and **1200 seconds including loading**. No warmup, smoke, benchmark, calibration, transfer or retry. Prior runtime does not certify completion of this different method; timeout is INCONCLUSIVE.

The new recording policy reuses unchanged audited locking/capture/writer foundations with scoped artifacts and whole-record admission. The complete content ceiling is **512 MiB**, with **16 MiB complete auxiliary allowance** and a **1 GiB free-space preguard**. Categories sum to 524,995,016 bytes: logits 214,616,520; rows 226,492,416; updates 67,108,864; auxiliary 16,777,216. Rows are capped at 1 MiB per complete record, updates at 8 MiB; logit files are limited to the exact 216 scheduled names and individual compressed bound. Auxiliary includes raw log, events, endpoint, candidate/report/audit, receipts, scratch, partial-overlap accounting and inventory itself. Detailed tests and serializer bounds belong to the exact-source preparation certificate.

Merged binary worker output is capped at 4 MiB with 65,536-byte reads. A prefix, overflow, unknown unread tail, failed termination or unjoined writer is explicitly INCONCLUSIVE. No mutable journals are read before confirmed quiescence. Reserved failure/finalization admission, one final inventory, no post-seal writes/normalization, and read-only no-repair verification are required. This is a cooperative Windows file-content bound, not an OS sandbox or bound on RAM, filesystem metadata or external sync copies. Unquiescent/failed I/O may prevent a full inventory; receipt persistence is then best effort and never represented as a complete seal.
